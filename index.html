HereDo Privacy Policy

Effective 18 November 2026 · HereDo · Earntelecom@yahoo.com

This policy explains how HereDo ("we", "us") handles information when you use the HereDo mobile app and related services. HereDo is a marketplace for finding and booking beauty services, shopping products, chatting with vendors, managing orders, and receiving vendor payouts.

Information we collect

  • Account and profile: email address, password (stored as a hash), name, username, first and last name, role (client or vendor), profile photo, and a location you choose to enter.
  • Vendor verification and payouts: bank account details, BVN/NIN or other identity details you submit, and verification images.
  • Payments and wallet: Paystack transaction references, payment status, amounts, wallet balance, payout and transfer records, and order history.
  • Marketplace content: service and product listings, prices, descriptions, images, feed posts, comments, bookmarks, and saved items.
  • Communications: messages between users, support tickets, and app notifications.
  • Orders and delivery: items ordered, order status, and shipping address you provide.

How we use information

We use this information to create and secure accounts, show and manage listings, process bookings and orders, enable chat, deliver notifications, verify vendors, process payments and payouts through Paystack, prevent fraud, respond to support requests, and comply with legal obligations.

Information we share

We do not sell personal information and do not use advertising networks, advertising IDs, analytics SDKs, or crash-reporting SDKs.

  • Supabase provides authentication, database, file storage, and server-side functions. It processes account, profile, content, message, order, and wallet data on our behalf.
  • Paystack processes payments, resolves bank details, and sends transfers to vendors. It receives the information needed to complete those transactions, such as email, amount, payment reference, and bank or account details for payouts.
  • We may disclose information when required by law or to protect the rights, safety, and integrity of HereDo, our users, and the public.

Encryption and security

User data is transmitted between the app and our servers over HTTPS/TLS. Access to privileged operations is restricted through server-side authentication, row-level security, role checks, and webhook signature verification.

Retention and account deletion

We keep account and profile data while your account is active. To request deletion, follow the steps on our account deletion page. We verify the request using the email address on your account and complete deletion within 30 days, except where records must be retained by law or for fraud, dispute, or chargeback resolution. Transaction and ledger records and records needed for legal compliance are retained for the applicable statutory period and then deleted or anonymised.

Your choices

You can update your profile and listings in the app and request account deletion at any time using the account deletion page. You may also contact us for privacy questions or data requests.

Children

HereDo is intended for users aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
