Delete your HereDo account

HereDo · Privacy policy · Earntelecom@yahoo.com

If you want HereDo to delete your account and associated personal data, follow these steps. You do not need to delete your account inside the app first.

  1. Send an email to Earntelecom@yahoo.com with the subject Delete my HereDo account.
  2. Use the email address registered to your HereDo account and state whether you are a client or vendor. You may include your username or user ID, but it is not required.
  3. We will verify that the request came from the account email address and may ask one short confirmation question before processing it.
  4. We will confirm receipt within 7 days and complete the deletion within 30 days, unless records must be retained for legal, fraud, dispute, or chargeback purposes.
Data deleted: account and profile details, username, photos you uploaded, listings, feed posts, comments, bookmarks, messages, support-ticket content, notification records, user-entered location, shipping address, and identity-verification images where law does not require retention.

Data retained: transaction, wallet, payout, audit, and ledger records needed to complete payouts, resolve disputes, prevent fraud, or meet legal and tax obligations. Retained records are deleted or anonymised after the applicable retention period.

Need help?

Email Earntelecom@yahoo.com with any questions about this process or to request a copy of the data we hold about you.
